Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about CampusConnect features.

How do I mark my attendance at an event?

Log in to your User Dashboard and click Attendance QR in the sidebar. Select your registered event to display your unique Student Dummy QR Pass. Present this screen to the Event Host or Coordinator at the venue, who will scan it with their camera scanner to mark you as Present.

How do I provide feedback for an event?

Once an event you participated in is completed, open your User Dashboard and click Give Feedback. Select the completed event from the dropdown, choose your rating from 1 to 5 stars, pick a category (e.g., Organization, Venue, Content), and share your comments. Your submitted review will appear in your My Feedback history table.

How can I register for an upcoming event?

From the User Dashboard, click Register Event or click Register next to any event under Upcoming Events. Fill in your student information and department, then submit. The event will immediately appear in your My Registered Events list.

How do Hosts and Coordinators scan QR passes?

Hosts and Coordinators can open Scan Attendance (or Students / Attendance) from their respective dashboards. Click Start Camera to activate the scanner, allow browser camera permissions, and point the camera at the student's Dummy QR Pass. The system verifies the student and marks them present in real time.

How can a Host publish a new campus event?

Log in with Host credentials and click Create Event in the sidebar. Fill in the event title, date, venue/room, and description, then click Publish Event. Your event will be listed for students to browse and register.

Who has permission to upload photos to the gallery?

To maintain content quality, photo uploads are restricted to authorized Host and Coordinator accounts. Students can browse all event photos in the public Event Gallery.

What are the demo login credentials for CampusConnect?

CampusConnect includes four built-in demo roles for testing:
• Admin: admin / admin123
• Coordinator: coord / coord123
• Host: host / host123
• Student / User: user / user123
